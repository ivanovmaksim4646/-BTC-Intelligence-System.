# Historical replay v1
Offline research on the verified OKX snapshot and genuine archived TradingView events. Every historical evaluation uses M5/H1/H4 prefixes closed by that timestamp, with 30-bar warmup. Real alerts must have been received within 360 seconds of their close and within the snapshot cutoff. Revalidation uses historical event close as a simulated clock; this is not proof of actual live data availability at that instant. No synthetic alerts. The existing paper gate is reused without relaxing its rules. Only later M5 candles evaluate hypothetical fixed-R outcomes; unresolved and same-bar ambiguous cases are censored. Outputs are isolated from forward paper journals. Initial snapshot contains approximately one day of M5 history; this is not a representative performance study. No full coverage or profitability claim. Run manually through the dedicated workflow; no new recurring compute or paid service.
